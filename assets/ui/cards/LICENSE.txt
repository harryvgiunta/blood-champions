IRONWOOD REWARD CARD ART - LICENSE AND PROVENANCE

Everything under this directory (assets/ui/cards/) is built by one command:

    python tools/build_reward_cards.py

which runs Blender 5.1 in background mode on tools/prepare_reward_cards.py.
cards.json records the Blender version, the seed, the 9-slice margin and the
digest of every render. Each file is a Cycles render of real geometry - a
beveled metal ring with a cut stone in each corner, a solid family emblem, a
world plaque - so the card's texture is the render and there is no bake step.
No downloaded art is used; the geometry and the materials are authored by that
script.

WHAT IS HERE

  frame_<rarity>.png         the card's plate, idle
  frame_<rarity>_lit.png     the same plate under the pointer's own light
  sigil_<family>.png         one emblem per upgrade family, on transparency
  plaque_<rarity>.png        the plate a world prop hangs its name on
  cards.json                 the build record and the manifest the HUD reads

Rarities: common, epic, legendary, greed, neutral (the fallback a promise with
no tier wears). Families: shot, hand, ability, perk, relic.

The frames are 9-slice plates: the left and right strips are averaged down their
length and the top and bottom across theirs, so a stretched edge never smears,
while the four corner squares and the centre keep their detail. The patch margin
in cards.json is the border in texture pixels and in drawn pixels at once -
NinePatchRect does not scale its margins - so the plate is drawn at 1:1.
