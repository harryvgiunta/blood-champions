IRONWOOD REWARD CHEST - LICENSE AND PROVENANCE

Everything under this directory (assets/props/chests/) is built by one command:

    python tools/build_reward_chests.py

which runs Blender 5.1 in background mode on tools/prepare_reward_chests.py.
reward_chests.json records the Blender version, the seed, the digest of every
output and every texture map, and the measured bounds of the three variants.
No downloaded model, scan or texture is used: the geometry, the analytic surface
fields and the Cycles bakes are all authored by that script from nothing, so
there is no upstream licence to carry and no source pack to stage.

WHAT IS HERE

  reward_chests.glb              Chest_Island, Chest_Cache, Chest_Hoard
  reward_chests.json             the build record
  textures/chest_<group>_*.png   the shipped maps, one set per material:
                                 albedo (sRGB, pigment only), tangent normal
                                 (OpenGL), ORM (R = occlusion, G = roughness,
                                 B = metallic). chest_gem_emit.png is the only
                                 emissive map; the runtime tints that surface to
                                 the chest's tier.

CONVENTIONS

  axes      Godot +Y up; every chest stands on its origin and its front faces -Z
  albedo    pigment only - no light, shadow or glare is baked into a map
  normal    OpenGL tangent space (+Y green up), which is what glTF and Godot read
