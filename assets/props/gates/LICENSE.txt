IRONWOOD GATE - LICENSE AND PROVENANCE

Everything under this directory (assets/props/gates/) is built by one command:

    python tools/build_gate_assets.py

which runs Blender 5.1 in background mode on tools/prepare_gate_assets.py.
ironwood_gates.json records the Blender version, the seed, the digest of every
output and every texture map, and the measured bounds of the three doors. No
downloaded model, scan or texture is used: the geometry, the analytic surface
fields and the Cycles bakes are authored by that script from nothing.

WHAT IS HERE

  ironwood_gates.glb          Gate_Watchers, Gate_Demons, Gate_Clones
  ironwood_gates.json         the build record
  textures/gate_<group>_*.png the shipped maps, one set per material:
                              granite (2048), basalt, bone, flesh and iron (1024
                              each). Albedo is sRGB pigment only, the normal is
                              OpenGL tangent space, and ORM is R occlusion,
                              G roughness, B metallic.

CONVENTIONS

  axes      Godot +Y up; every gate stands on its origin and faces -Z
  opening   2.2 m wide by 2.9 m tall - the runtime lays the biome's sheet in it
  albedo    pigment only - no light, shadow or glare is baked into a map

The glowing sheet is not in this asset: its colour is the biome's promise, so
scripts/roguelite/gate_visual.gd draws it and owns the lamp.
